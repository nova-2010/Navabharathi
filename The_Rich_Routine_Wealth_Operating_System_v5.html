Assumptions

Make the model yours

FIRE corpus = desired annual lifestyle ÷ withdrawal rate. Default example: ₹4L/month × 12 ÷ 4% ≈ ₹12Cr. Actual results depend on savings, returns, inflation, taxes, sequence of returns and future lifestyle.

Financial principles

Rules the system follows

Expense ≠ every transaction.
Investment, saving, transfers and debt payments are tracked separately.
Goal contributions ≠ lifestyle expenses.
Special goals are funded before monthly allocation.
Actual ≠ target ≠ projection.
Reports keep those concepts visually distinct.
